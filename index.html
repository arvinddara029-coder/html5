Super expansion · Legacy Collection & Anokolisa

Legacy Collection (Gothicvania, TinyRPG, Warped, Misc and Explosions/Magic) supplies new actors, spell effects, backgrounds and terrain. Anokolisa’s High Forest and Debug Map contribute character/mob animation, trees and tiles. Original license and source documents remain in assets/super.

Mario’s Madness model contributors

FunkyBunny, DarksArtworks and Sharkman; thanks to Marco Antonio and the Mario’s Madness team. Eleven supplied FBX exports were rendered into four-angle guardian sprites. Original project artwork

Operator portraits are original SVGs. Hero, city, garden and reactor background paintings are AI-generated. Effects and procedural scenery are drawn in code.

Typography & music

Barlow Condensed, DM Sans, Orbitron and Rajdhani are bundled locally under their SIL Open Font Licenses. The soundtrack and remaining combat sounds are synthesized with Web Audio.
