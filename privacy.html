PRIVACY POLICY

NEON RONIN · PROTOCOL ZERO
Last updated: 28 September 2026

1 · THE SHORT VERSION

This game has no account system, no server of its own, and no tracking of ours. Your progress stays in your browser (or in your CrazyGames account if cloud save is available). Multiplayer matches run directly between players — we never see or store your match data.

2 · DATA THE GAME STORES ON YOUR DEVICE

The game saves the following locally (browser storage / localStorage on your device):

  • Profile: callsign, level, XP, currency, achievements and records
  • Progress: unlocked worlds, owned equipment, cosmetics, Vault items and settings (audio, HUD, controls)
  • Run state: checkpoints and the last saved survival wave, so you can resume after a reload

This data never leaves your device unless you use the platform's cloud save (see below). Clearing your site data removes it permanently. Nothing in the game fingerprints your device or builds an advertising profile.

3 · CRAZYGAMES PLATFORM

When the game runs on CrazyGames, the official CrazyGames SDK may handle:

  • Cloud save — your progress is synced to your CrazyGames account so it follows you between devices. This is optional and only active when you are logged in to CrazyGames.
  • Ads — advertising is served by CrazyGames under their own privacy policy. The game itself does not embed ad networks or read ad identifiers.
  • Username & avatar — shown in the lobby and used as your in-game name when you are signed in.
  • Room & invite links — used to let you invite friends into your multiplayer room.
  • Gameplay events (loading / gameplay start & stop) — required by the platform to time ads correctly.

See the CrazyGames Privacy Policy for details on how the platform processes data.

4 · MULTIPLAYER, CHAT & VOICE

Online rooms use direct peer-to-peer WebRTC connections between players, coordinated through a public peer broker (PeerJS) only to exchange connection handshakes. Match positions, chat messages and voice audio travel player-to-player — they are not recorded or stored by us.

  • Text chat can be moderated by the platform's chat preferences; mute / block / report controls are built in.
  • Voice chat is strictly opt-in. The microphone is only requested after you press the voice button, audio streams only while you hold push-to-talk, and stopping voice chat releases the microphone immediately.

5 · WHAT WE DO NOT COLLECT

  • No real names, emails or phone numbers
  • No precise location, contacts, or camera access
  • No third-party analytics or marketing trackers of our own
  • No sale or sharing of player data — because we never receive it

6 · CHILDREN & SAFETY

The game is suitable for a general audience. It contains no chat with strangers unless you actively join an online room, and voice chat requires a deliberate action. If you are a parent, you can clear all local progress at any time from your browser settings.

7 · CHANGES & CONTACT

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date. Questions, requests or reports can be raised through the game's publisher page on CrazyGames or through the repository's issue tracker.
